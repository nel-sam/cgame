# cgame (Dragon Puppy Fun)

## The app

During one of my trips to visit my sister's family in the gloomy north I explained to my then 9 year old niece what it was that I did for work. When I mentioned I built phone apps, her eyes opened wide and she asked "can you make games?!?". Well at that point I had never made a game but I already was good at teaching myself anything in software development, so I asked her to give me a couple hours to investigate. A couple hours later I had a decent understanding of how the [Flame API](https://pub.dev/documentation/flame/latest/) works with Flutter and we got to work. My niece made all the artwork (except the water and clouds) and I wrote the code while showing her the logic I used so that she could have a soft intro into coding. Her little sister was starting to get interested in playing piano/keyboard and she contributed by making the music loop using [AudioKit Synth One](https://audiokitpro.com/synth/) on her iPad. The music was made with Synth One's excellent arpeggiator.

## The learning

The goal was to teach my nieces about my cool job, how games are (sometimes) made, and to gain some cool uncle points. I'm still waiting for confirmation that the 3 goals were accomplished. 

## Future for the app

This app will just get Flutter and library updates as needed, but any future games will start as a brand new project.
